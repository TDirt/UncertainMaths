## Slide 1: The Problem

### Entrepreneurs Make Decisions in the Dark

- **Current reality:** Single-value inputs in calculators
- **The cost:** Overestimate → rejection; Underestimate → lost money
- **The gap:** No accessible tools for uncertainty quantification
- **The irony:** Even Excel makes Monte Carlo analysis painful

> "90% of business decisions are made with no understanding of the risk."

## Slide 2: The Solution

### Decyze — Decision-Making for Everyone

**Tagline:** "Eliminate guesswork: make fast business decisions knowing your chances of being right."

**What we build:**
- Mobile uncertainty calculator with confidence intervals
- AI-assisted business model builder
- Value of Information (VoI) analysis
- Export to Excel for collaboration

**Key differentiator:** Invisible probability distributions — no statistics expertise required
