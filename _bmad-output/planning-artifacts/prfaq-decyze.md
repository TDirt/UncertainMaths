## 2. Problem Statement

### The Pain
- Entrepreneurs and sales professionals make decisions with single values, ignoring uncertainty
- They use smartphone calculators, gut feelings, or Excel
- Cost of being wrong: overestimating → rejection; underestimating → lost money & time

### The Gap
- Existing tools (@RISK, Crystal Ball) require statistical expertise
- Too expensive and complex for mainstream adoption
- Output too technical for steering meetings
- LLMs can help but can't verify Monte Carlo simulations

## 5. Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors
- @RISK (Palisade)
- Crystal Ball (Oracle)
- Pigment

### Key Differentiator
- **Invisible probability distributions** — MetaLog as default
- No statistical expertise required
- Mobile-first vs. desktop enterprise

### Real Competitors (Hidden)
- **Excel & PowerPoint** — the default decision tools
- **Internal politics** — gut-based decision making
- **LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude)** — emerging alternative

### Why Incumbents Failed
- Too complex for decision analysts to produce fast enough
- Output too technical for steering meetings
- Require user to know probability distribution types
